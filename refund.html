Refund & Return Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

EZ Customz installs custom work in your vehicle: starlight headliners, ambient lighting and speaker builds. Because every job is built and installed to order, our policy is simple: payments are final, but we stand behind the parts we install. This policy works together with our Deposit & Payment Terms.

1. Deposits are nonrefundable

All deposits paid to EZ Customz are nonrefundable. A deposit reserves your appointment and secures your requested services, and it is applied toward the total cost of the agreed-upon work.

2. No refunds on completed work

Once services are completed and paid for, payments are not refunded. Parts and kits installed in your vehicle cannot be returned for a refund.

3. Faulty kits and parts are replaced

If a kit or part we supplied and installed turns out to be faulty, we will replace it at no cost to you. This covers things like:

  • Starlight kits, light engines or fiber that stop working.
  • Ambient lighting kits, LED strips or controllers that fail.
  • Other components we supplied that are defective.

Replacement is our remedy for faulty parts, instead of a refund.

4. What isn't covered

  • Parts or kits supplied by the customer.
  • Damage from accidents, misuse, water, or electrical problems in the vehicle not caused by our work.
  • Parts that were removed, modified or repaired by anyone other than EZ Customz.
  • Changes of mind about a style, color or design after the work is done. We're happy to quote changes as new work.

5. How to request a replacement

Call or text 225.615.4554 and describe the problem, with photos or a short video if you can. We'll set up a time for you to bring the vehicle to the shop so we can check it and replace the faulty part.

6. Your rights under the law

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or other rights you have under applicable law.
